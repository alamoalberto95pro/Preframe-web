What PreFrame is

PreFrame helps you plan the next story you want to tell — whether you already know the song, or you don't yet.

If you have the track, drop it in and feel it: a focused mode strips away everything else so you can listen, sense where the shots live, and mark cuts as you go. If you don't have it yet, sketch the shape of the piece as you imagine it and build the story around that instead.

Either way, switch to a structured mode to turn what you felt — or imagined — into a real plan: section by section, shot by shot. A moodboard runs alongside you through it all, so the look of the piece never gets lost. And when the real track finally lands, neither does your plan.

Who made this

Behind PreFrame

I'm Alberto: a programmer by trade and by hobby — coming up on a decade building software — and a filmmaker purely for the fun of it: I film my own travels :). PreFrame comes from realising that filmmakers have no dedicated place to bring our ideas to light: inspiration lives in scattered notes, gear in lists, timing in spreadsheets. PreFrame is that place, and it grows by listening to what filmmakers of every level need — hobbyist to pro.
