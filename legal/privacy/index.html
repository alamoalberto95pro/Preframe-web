6. This website

The Preframe website (preframe-app.com) is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (United States). Like any web server, GitHub keeps technical logs of the requests it receives, including the visitor's IP address, in order to deliver and secure the service. That processing is GitHub's own and is governed by their privacy policy.

This site uses no cookies and no browser storage, which is why you are never asked to accept anything. Every font is served from this same domain: no requests are made to Google Fonts or to advertising networks. The only request to a third party is the cookieless analytics script described in section 7.

7. Website analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., to measure its audience in aggregate. It records page views together with the referring site, the country, the browser and device type, and page performance metrics. It does not record clicks on individual buttons or links.

The measurement is cookieless: it stores nothing in your browser, uses no persistent identifier, does not fingerprint your device and does not follow you across other websites. Visitors are never identified individually and no profile is built about you. That is why this site still asks you to accept nothing.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing whether the site works and reaches the people it is meant for (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
